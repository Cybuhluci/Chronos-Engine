# Echoes of The Eternity

Not a studio project (just so you know). Just me messing around with Unity to make a Fallout Doctor Who game. Welcome.

### Quick pitch
A Fallout Who Vegas inspired game, where you go around to 7 unique, nicely-sized, open worlds to follow a main quest, and do many of the silly side-quests, as well as discover the secret story hidden under the surface.

### Core things to know
- Scope: Experimental prototype. No story, no missions yet.  
- Player: You are a camera-controlled player with no body model in the current build.  
- TARDIS: Interior exists and about no buttons work right now, since the focus is on the actual open world gameplay.
- Assets: Prototype assets and placeholders. Expect rough models and temporary sounds.  
- Spoilers: There isn't much to spoil yet. So dont worry about finding them.

### Controls & play notes
- Default controls: keyboard + mouse; WASD to move, mouse to look, E to interact with stuff.  
- When reporting bugs: open an issue and include reproduction steps and a short video if possible.

### Status
Very early prototype. Current priorities:
- Creating a blasted dialogue system that can handle branching conversations and quests.
- Creating a basic open world with some NPCs to test the dialogue system and world interactions.
